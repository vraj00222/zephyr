ZEPHYR — DEMO SCRIPT
one straight line: site tour -> tech line -> press a paper -> read it -> poster -> close
plain lines = speak. (parentheses) = what to do.
setup: dev server on, localhost:3000 open, zoom 100%, notifications off, mic allowed, w9.pdf on desktop.

--------------------------------------------------

INTRO
(homepage hero, still)

Hi, I'm Vraj. This is Zephyr.

The mistral is the strong wind of southern France.
The zephyr is its gentle sibling — the west wind.
Mistral's models do the heavy work underneath.
Zephyr is the breeze that carries research to you.


THE PROBLEM

AI moves faster than anyone can read.
The best engineers are the ones who still read the papers.
But papers are two tiny columns, figures pages away from
their mention. So most of us just... stopped reading them.

Zephyr makes papers people actually read.


THE SITE
(scroll slowly, one line per section, keep moving)

Paste a link or drop a PDF. No account.

(why-zephyr cards)
The issue — reading papers hurts.
The solution — we re-set them like a first edition.
The magic — you just read.

(poster wall)
Refresh. Zephyr. Look up. Think.
Reading should feel like this — not like homework.

(features)
Figures move. Citations become links. Print ready.

(rooms walk — CLICK the grand hall plate)
You even pick the room you read in.
I'll take the grand hall. Every paper opens there now.

(bento gallery — just point, don't click)
These are real papers, already pressed.

(press-run numbers)
Today alone — eight papers, half a million characters read,
two hundred claims checked.

(scroll back up to the input box)


THE TECH, ONE LINE

Under the hood it's Mistral end to end.
OCR reads every page. The chat models re-write and
proofread. Voxtral listens and speaks. That's it.


THE DOORKEEPER
(click the paperclip in the input box, upload w9.pdf)

First — what if I hand it a tax form?

(wait for the joke, read it out loud)

Papers only. Everything else, coming soon.


THE PRESS
(paste arxiv.org/abs/2310.06825 and hit Typeset it)

Now the real thing — Mistral's own paper.

(press runs, cat walks — stay quiet a moment)

Receive. Read. Re-set. Bind.


THE EDITION
(it lands on the paper — point at the badge)

Done. Thirty claims checked against the original.

(click Octavo)

Three cuts of every paper. This is Octavo — half the length.
Every orange highlight is the paper's own sentence,
verified character by character. We don't trust the model —
we check it.

(click Pamphlet, one beat)

And Pamphlet — the five minute brief, with a verdict.

(scroll to the architecture engraving)

The press even draws the architecture.

(select a sentence — click Ask Le Chat)

Highlight anything, and ask.

(press cmd-option — cat meows — click the mic and say:)

"What are three things to remember from this paper?"

(click mic again — answer streams, then SPEAKS —
let the voice play a few seconds, don't talk over it)

Voice in. Voice out. All Mistral.


THE POSTER
(click the Poster button, let it flip in)

And when you're done reading — every paper becomes a poster.


CLOSE

Next: a subscription. One pressed paper a day, in your field.

Zephyr. Papers people actually read.

IF THEY ASK

Moat? Everyone summarizes. We prove every highlight and
check every claim. Receipts on screen.

Hallucination? A second model proofreads the first, fixes
what it can, and the badge hides itself when unsure.

Time and cost? Two to four minutes. Pennies.

What breaks? Very long papers get cut at 120k characters.
Heavy math confuses the proofreader — and it says so.

Vibe? Co-authored commits, code review, prompts.
Check: git log, search "Mistral Vibe".
